Decluttering

A Realistic Weekend Decluttering Plan for a Small Home

A short, realistic plan works better than trying to empty the entire home at once.

Friday: choose a target

Pick one drawer, shelf, or corner. Gather a donation bag and a box for items that belong elsewhere.

Saturday: work in short rounds

Set a timer for 20 minutes, then take a break.

Use four decisions

Keep, relocate, donate, or discard.

Finish one surface

A clear table, counter, or shelf gives you a visible result.

Sunday: remove outgoing items

Take donations and recycling out instead of leaving bags in a hallway.

Create a maintenance habit

Choose a daily time to return items and clear one surface.

Start small

Progress does not require a perfect home in one weekend.

Quick reminder: Measure first, follow product instructions, and secure shelves or tall furniture safely.
