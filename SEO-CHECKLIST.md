# SEO launch checklist
- Replace the placeholder Netlify domain in sitemap.xml and robots.txt after deployment.
- Each article has a unique title and meta description.
- Add original or licensed photos and descriptive alt text.
- Add contextual internal links between related articles as the site grows.
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
- Check mobile layout, broken links, spelling, and page titles.
- No traffic, ranking, advertising approval, or income is guaranteed.
